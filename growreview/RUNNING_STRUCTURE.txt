BEST UPDATE ORDER FOR YOUR CURRENT HOSTINGER SITE
1. Backup existing DB.
2. Import upgrade-v5.sql.
3. Upload/overwrite V5 files.
4. Do not overwrite .env.
5. Clear Hostinger/CDN cache.
6. Test /api/health.php.
7. Open /super-admin.
